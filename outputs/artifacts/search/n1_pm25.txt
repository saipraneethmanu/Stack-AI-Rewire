The European Environment Agency (EEA) and regional environmental agencies (ARPA) in Italy provide public access to historic air quality measurements.

---

### Primary Source: European Environment Agency (EEA) – Air Quality e-Reporting / AirBase

* **Geographic Coverage:** All Italian regions and major cities (e.g., Milan, Rome, Turin, Florence, Bologna).
* **Parameters & Resolution:** Hourly monitoring station time series for $\text{NO}_2$ and $\text{PM}_{2.5}$ (along with $\text{PM}_{10}$, $\text{O}_3$, $\text{SO}_2$, and $\text{CO}$).
* **Temporal Coverage:** Multi-decadal records, including **2004–2005** (covered under historical AirBase / e-Reporting time series).
* **Download Portal:** 
  * [EEA Air Quality Download Service](https://eeadmz1-downloads-webapp.azurewebsites.net/) *(allows filtering by Country: Italy / `IT`, Pollutant: `PM2.5` / `NO2`, Year: `2004` & `2005`, and aggregation level: hourly).*
  * Alternatively accessible programmatically via the [EEA Air Quality Portal & API](https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/datahub/datahubitem-view/0e2f9d6c-6a3a-44e2-9ef8-e04e4a6a57c2) or the Python client `airbase`.
* **Format:** Parquet / CSV.
* **Licence:** Open data under **CC BY 4.0** (or standard EEA Data Policy: free reuse for commercial and non-commercial purposes with attribution).

---

### Alternative / Regional Source: Regione Lombardia / ARPA Lombardia Open Data

For cities such as **Milan**, **Bergamo**, and **Brescia**, ARPA Lombardia maintains granular historic measurements via the regional open data infrastructure:

* **Download Page:** [Dati sensori aria – Open Data Regione Lombardia](https://www.dati.lombardia.it/Ambiente/Dati-sensori-aria/nicp-bhqi)
* **Coverage:** Long-term historical station monitoring for Lombardy. Note that while $\text{NO}_2$ is continuously logged at hourly intervals, older $\text{PM}_{2.5}$ data in Italian networks is frequently reported as 24-hour daily gravimetric/continuous averages (if true hourly $\text{PM}_{2.5}$ is strictly required, query automated optical/TEOM monitor stations via the EEA portal above).
* **Licence:** **Creative Commons Zero / CC0 1.0 Universal** (Public Domain Dedication).
