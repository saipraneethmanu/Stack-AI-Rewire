The most comprehensive, continuously updated public dataset matching your criteria is provided by **ARPA Lombardia** (the Environmental Protection Agency of Lombardia) covering the **Metropolitan City of Milan** and surrounding urban/regional areas in Northern Italy.

---

### 1. Dataset Overview

* **Provider / Custodian:** ARPA Lombardia / Regione Lombardia.
* **Network & Coverage:** Urban and peri-urban monitoring stations across the city network (e.g., central Milan stations like *Milano Brera, Milano Lambrate, Milano Juvara, Milano Zavattari*, etc.) as well as the wider regional network.
* **Temporal Resolution:** Hourly observations (timestamped in Solar Time / UTC+1).
* **Time Span:** Available continuously for 2024–2025 (historical annual archives + real-time/monthly rolling feeds).
* **Observed Parameters:**
  * **Temperature** (°C)
  * **Relative Humidity** (%)
  * **Wind Speed & Wind Direction** (m/s and degrees)
  * *(Also includes global solar radiation, precipitation, atmospheric pressure, and peak wind gusts)*.

### 3. Download Pages & Access Endpoints

ARPA Lombardia publishes these feeds directly through the open data portal of Lombardy:

1. **Main Meteorological Data Portal & Downloads:**
   * **Catalog Hub:** [ARPA Lombardia - Dati di Monitoraggio (Dati.Lombardia.it)](https://www.dati.lombardia.it/stories/s/auv9-c2sj)
   * **Rolling/Recent Sensor Dataset:** [Dati sensori meteo (Open Data Regione Lombardia)](https://www.dati.lombardia.it/Ambiente/Dati-sensori-meteo/647i-nhxk)
     * Offers instant export in **CSV, JSON, XML**, and programmatic access via the **Socrata Open Data API (SODA) / OData**.

2. **Annual Historical Archives (2024–2025):**
   * Archived yearly files for previous and validated years are indexed directly under the [Dati di monitoraggio repository](https://www.dati.lombardia.it/stories/s/auv9-c2sj) split by year (e.g., *2024*, *2025*).

3. **Station Metadata & Geographic Locations:**
   * **Stations & Sensor Registry:** [Stazioni e Sensori ARPA Lombardia](https://www.dati.lombardia.it/d/nf78-nj6b)
   * Contains exact latitude, longitude, elevation, sensor IDs, and station names (such as the Milano urban stations) to map the IDs from the weather observations file.

4. **Interactive Custom Extraction Tool (Alternative):**
   * **ARPA Lombardia Automatic Data Request Tool:** [ARPA Lombardia - Richiesta Dati Misurati](https://www.arpalombardia.it/temi-ambientali/meteo-e-clima/richiesta-dati-misurati/)
   * Allows querying specific individual city stations (e.g., *Milano - Via Juvara* or *Milano - Brera*) and downloading specific multi-parameter CSVs for 2024–2025 directly.
